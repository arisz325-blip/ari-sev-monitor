List vs model report. An entry on the Specialist and Enthusiast Vehicles Register means that model variant may be imported through a Registered Automotive Workshop. A workshop can only actually bring one in once it holds an in-force model report for it — that is the green badge, and the workshop named on the card is who holds it. "No model report" means the car is on the list but nobody is set up to comply it yet.

Neither is an import approval by itself — you still apply for that, and vehicles built before 1989 or over 25 years old have their own pathways. Always confirm against ROVER before you buy.
